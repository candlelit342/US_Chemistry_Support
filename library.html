John Gould · 1837–1848 · Published in London

The Birds of Europe

Gould's Birds of Europe was issued in five volumes, each a physical folio of hand-coloured lithographic plates. This is a digital shelf for that set — each volume gets its own gallery, its own title page, and its own record of how ornithology has changed since Gould drew it. Two volumes are open so far; the rest are on the shelf, waiting.
